# Pokémon Pink beta walkthrough

A story walkthrough of what should happen in the Pokémon Pink cart, in the
order you'll play it. It isn't a full guide to Kanto: anything not listed here
should play the way it does in Pokémon Yellow. Use it to check what you see,
and tick things off as you go.

Each line says what to do and what should happen. Lines marked **expected**
describe something that's meant to be that way, so please don't report those.

## Reporting a bug

Post in the Pink thread on Discord or open an issue on GitHub with:
- where you were (map, and roughly where on it)
- what happened, and what this guide says should have happened
- your COLORS setting (RED++, OG YELLOW, SGB...) for anything colour-related
- a screenshot or a short clip if you can
- the cart version from the launcher (this guide is for 0.1.0-beta.1)

Your partner is Clefairy throughout. Where Yellow says Pikachu, Pink says
Clefairy, and the real Pikachu is a normal wild Pokémon again.

## Title and intro

- [ ] Start the cart and let the intro play: Clefairy falls in, surfs, stands up, appears close up, flies through the clouds and jumps. Clefairy should be pink in every scene, never orange, purple or blue.
- [ ] The title drops in with Pink's logo (POKéMON, "Clefa!", PINK VERSION) and Clefairy. No yellow square should flash on Clefairy's face as it lands.
- [ ] Clefairy blinks on the title screen.
- [ ] With the `pink_sgb_border.zip` skin chosen, the border shows Clefairys, FAIRY bubbles and the POKéMON and PINK lettering around the game.

## New game and Pallet Town

- [ ] Oak's speech shows Clefairy (not Nidorino or Pikachu), coloured pink.
- [ ] Just before "First, what is your name?", Oak asks whether you'd like to pick your look. YES opens PRISM; back out of it and Oak carries on with your new look. **Expected:** this is a cart extra, not in Pink.
- [ ] Walk into the tall grass on Route 1: Oak stops you and catches a wild Clefairy.
- [ ] In the lab, your rival snatches the other Poké Ball. You receive Clefairy.
- [ ] Rival battle: he sends out CHARMANDER (L5). **Expected:** Pink gives the rival the manga's Charmander line all game, whatever happens in this fight.
- [ ] Try to put Clefairy in its ball: it pops back out and follows you.
- [ ] The follower is a pink, animated Clefairy.
- [ ] Clefairy's voice plays in place of Pikachu's (talk to it, check its mood).

These swaps are Pink's and apply everywhere. **Expected**, so please don't
report them as missing Pokémon:
- Sandshrew and Sandslash appear as Ekans and Arbok; Grimer and Muk as Koffing and Weezing; Venonat and Venomoth as Meowth and Persian; Drowzee as Mr. Mime; Growlithe as Vulpix (Growlithe is a Game Corner prize).
