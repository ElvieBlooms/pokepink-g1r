## Pokémon Pink Version: Special Clefairy Edition
- **jcferggy**, original romhack: design, data, scripts and events.
  Original release: [Pokémon Pink Version: Special Clefairy Edition on PokéCommunity](https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/pok%C3%A9mon-pink-version-special-clefairy-edition-ue.542531/)

## Art in the original hack
- **jcferggy**: Pink's own tile edits (`mods/pink_tilesets/assets/pink_tiles.png`,
  59 tiles found in no Red, Blue, Yellow or Japanese Blue cart). Not covered by
  the MIT license; shipped with jcferggy's permission.
- **Artsy-Theo**: Clefairy emotion portraits (Pink's partner portraits are
  adapted from them; they ship as changed pixels in
  `mods/pink_presentation/art_data.lua`).
- **Sindorman**: the Clefairy animation that inspired the project (credited in
  Pink's README). Whether any of Sindorman's art is in the game: *to confirm.*
- Title screen art: *artist to confirm* (Pink's README says jcferggy coded the
  title animation but didn't draw it). The title Clefairy and its blink frames
  ship in `mods/pink_presentation/overrides/title/`; the logo edit ships as
  changed pixels.
- Clefairy voice clips (`mods/pink_voice/assets/cries/`): from Pokémon Pink;
  their original source isn't stated in Pink's release. *To confirm.*

These artists have not been contacted directly. jcferggy says he can't speak
for them, but in his experience they were fine with reuse as long as they are
credited. Their art is credited here and will be removed on request.

- **jcferggy**: the Busybody's and the Imposter's trainer pictures
  (`mods/pink_manga_events/assets/`), Pink's dialogue used in the mods, and
  Pink's sprite and picture edits (gym leaders, Elite Four, Giovanni, the
  manga characters, battle pictures, badges, slot reels, town map, the
  surfing minigame and surfing sprite, the bolt emote), which ship as changed pixels
  (`mods/pink_presentation/art_data.lua`) and are built over the player's own
  game art.
  Not covered by the MIT license.
