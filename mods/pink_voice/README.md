# Pink Voice

Pokémon Pink's Clefairy voice clips for Gen1Recomp (Yellow). Part of the
Pokémon Pink cart; needs **pink_partner**.

Yellow plays recorded Pikachu clips wherever your partner speaks: when you
talk to it, in its emotion portraits, on the title screen and in battle.
Pink replaces all 42 with Clefairy clips, and this mod plays them in the same
places.

Without this mod, `pink_partner` turns the recorded clips off and your
Clefairy uses its normal cry.

The engine loads the clips straight from the imported audio folder, so this
mod wraps its clip player to open the mod's files instead. That needs the
`engine_internals` permission.
