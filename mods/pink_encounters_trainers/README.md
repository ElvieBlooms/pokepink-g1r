# Pink Encounters & Trainers

Pokémon Pink's wild Pokémon, Super Rod tables and trainer teams for
Gen1Recomp (Yellow). Needs **pink_partner**.

- 36 maps' grass/water tables and 26 Super Rod tables, as in Pink
- trainer parties for 17 classes: manga Gym Leaders, the manga rival,
  Agatha's lone Clefable, generic trainers restored to Red/Blue
- Pink's hand-picked trainer moves

Rivals: Pink uses one team per rival fight, but vanilla Yellow's scripts
still choose a team by the rival's Eevee. Each vanilla team number is mapped
to Pink's team for that fight, so every branch gets Pink's team.

Only tables that differ from vanilla Yellow are included. `data.lua` is
generated; don't hand-edit it.

Not in this mod: the new manga trainers' names, sprites and events (CHIEF
and UNUSED_JUGGLER parties are here, but nothing uses them until the events
mod).
