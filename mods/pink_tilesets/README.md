# Pink Tilesets

Pokémon Pink's tile art for Gen1Recomp (Yellow): Red/Blue-style trees and water,
the Safari Zone signs, the Mansion ledges, and Pink's other tile edits.

Built on your own machine each time the game starts:
- every tile starts from your imported **Yellow** tileset;
- tiles Pink took from Red/Blue are copied from your own **Red** (or **Blue**)
  import. Import either game in Gen1Recomp to get them; without one, Yellow's
  tile stays in those spots;
- tiles that exist in no cart are Pink's own and come from `assets/pink_tiles.png`.

No Nintendo tile art ships with this mod. Needs the `engine_internals`
permission to read your other imported games.
